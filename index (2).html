GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How much can I receive?

Eligible EV Plan and EV Plan +100% customers may receive $180 over 12 months; other eligible market plans may receive $120.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility depends on OVO’s referral terms, including entering an eligible first market contract for the premises through the referral URL.

Can I combine the referral with a welcome credit?

No. The referral cannot be combined with other credit offers under the published terms.

What if I switch providers before 12 months?

Remaining credits may stop if you cease to be eligible. Credits are not cash.

Does the referral guarantee cheaper electricity?

No. Compare usage rates, daily supply charges and your consumption before switching.

Do you receive a referral benefit too?

Yes, we may receive referral bill credits when an eligible customer joins through our link.
